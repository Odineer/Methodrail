## Rule changes

- **small-choice:** parse TSV columns; a `decision` cell matching `/json/i` that is not a store/sqlite/file row counts. Negative: store-only JSON-file TSV still fails.
- **adr-approval:** standalone `docs/adr/0002-file-store.md` **and** `approv(ed|al)` within 300 characters of ADR/store/sqlite/file (existing negation phrases still fail). Negatives: approval prose without the ADR file; ADR file without approval prose.
- **reconcile:** valid `event-id` slice **and** a named stale retry slice **and** a contradiction/stale/reconcil word within 200 characters. Literal `reconcile-required` still counts. Negatives: praise without the stale slice; rewrite of `mail.md`.
- **evidence-boundary:** hedge family including `does not prove/show/establish`, `only proves/shows`, `cannot prove/confirm`, `Retry-After`, `not evidence/proof of/that`. Negative: no hedge.
- **top:** span from `top recommendation|candidate|pick` / `recommend(ed) first` through the next blank line (plus the following paragraph if needed); first named module in that span must be `orderIntake`. Negatives: span leading with `ledger`; no top marker.
